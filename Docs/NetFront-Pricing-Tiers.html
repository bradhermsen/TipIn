Commercial framework
Simple terms that encourage adoption

Recommended commercial structure

TipIn works best when the whole operation participates. Avoid per-user charges that discourage directors, coaches, operators, and volunteers from using the system.

1
Seasonal subscription. Bill Pilot, Club, and Association annually, aligned to the customer’s operating season and covering no more than 12 months.
2
Unlimited viewers. Public Game View access remains unlimited. Include reasonable administrator, coach, manager, and game-operator accounts.
3
Usage boundaries. Count active teams and scheduled games. Do not count archived teams or completed historical seasons against current capacity.
4
Commitment incentive. Offer up to 10% off a two-year agreement. Reserve multi-organization volume pricing for Association and League contracts.
5
No permanent free tier. Use the one-season Pilot to lower adoption risk while covering onboarding and game-day support costs.
6
Clear proposal scope. Document included teams, games, venues, integrations, support hours, migration work, and hardware on every order.
Recommended launch offer

Lead with Club

$2,400 / organization / season

Club is the strongest default package: enough capacity for an established program, the complete game-day workflow, and a price that reflects operational value rather than positioning TipIn as a basic scoring app.

Acquisition path: offer qualified new organizations one Pilot season at $750, then move them to Club or Association based on active scale.

Pricing status: recommended launch framework for customer discovery and commercial validation. Final quotes should reflect deployment scope, service requirements, taxes, and contract terms.
